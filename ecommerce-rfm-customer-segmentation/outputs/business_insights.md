# Key Business Insights

1. **High Value** customers are 22.8% of the customer base (1,341 customers) but generate 68.6% of total revenue (£11,924,494) — classic 80/20-style concentration.

2. **At Risk** customers (818 customers, 13.9% of the base) have historically generated £1,702,904 in revenue (9.8% of total) with an average spend of £2,082 each, but haven't purchased in 367 days on average — this is the single highest-value group to target with a win-back campaign.

3. The top 10 customers by revenue alone contribute £2,787,079 (16.0% of total revenue), underscoring how concentrated this business's revenue is among a small number of accounts.

4. **Churned** customers make up the largest single segment (1,532 customers, 26.1% of the base) but only 3.1% of revenue, with an average recency of 459 days — most were low-value to begin with (avg spend £354), so win-back spend is better directed at **At Risk** customers than at this group.

5. Recommendation: prioritize retention offers for the 818 **At Risk** customers (high historical value, lapsing now) over broad campaigns aimed at the larger but lower-value **Churned** segment, and protect the **High Value** segment's experience since it drives the majority of revenue from a minority of the customer base.
